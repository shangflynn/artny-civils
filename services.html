Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. We provide free site assessments and detailed, no-obligation quotes for all projects. Contact us to arrange a site visit.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Johannesburg South and serve the greater Gauteng region, including Sandton, Randburg, Roodepoort, Alberton, Soweto, Midrand, Centurion, Pretoria and Ekurhuleni.
Do you work on both residential and commercial projects?
Yes. We handle residential, commercial and industrial projects — from single driveways to large-scale paving and civil works.
How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines depend on scope and weather conditions. We'll give you a clear timeframe in your quote and keep you updated throughout.
Can you repair existing paving or surfaces?
Yes. We handle repairs and resurfacing of existing tar, brick paving, concrete and other surfaces. Send us photos via WhatsApp for a quick assessment.
