Road Construction

We provide road construction services for residential estates, commercial developments and infrastructure projects across Gauteng. From initial site preparation to final surfacing, we handle the full road construction process to specification.

Get a quote WhatsApp enquiry

What We Do

  • Estate and township road construction
  • Commercial development access roads
  • Industrial site roads and hardstands
  • Road widening and extensions
  • Road rehabilitation and resurfacing
  • Kerbing, channeling and drainage installation

Construction Process

  • Site survey and setting out
  • Earthworks — cut, fill and compaction to required levels
  • Sub-base and base layer installation with proper compaction
  • Drainage installation — stormwater pipes, culverts and channels
  • Kerbing and edging installation
  • Surface course — tar, asphalt or other specified surface
  • Road marking and signage where required

Project Capabilities

  • Residential estate roads and internal streets
  • Commercial and retail park access roads
  • Industrial hardstands and yards
  • Parking lot construction
  • Road maintenance and repairs
  • Stormwater management and drainage
